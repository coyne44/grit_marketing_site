GRIT's texting runs on your practice's **own local phone number** with two-way
messaging and automatic appointment reminders. Before any of that can work, US carriers
require your business to be registered with **The Campaign Registry (TCR)** — a
one-time process GRIT walks you through in three steps. The forms take a few minutes;
the approvals take longer, and **the waiting is normal**.

## Before you start

- Your **legal business name exactly as it appears on your IRS EIN filing** — a
  mismatch here is the #1 cause of rejection.
- Your **EIN**. (Sole proprietor without an EIN? There's a checkbox for that.)
- Your practice **website address**.

## Step 1 — Business verification

1. Go to **Settings → SMS**.
2. Fill in your legal business name, entity type, EIN, and website. If you already
   entered your EIN in practice settings, it's pre-filled.
3. Click **Submit to TCR**.

GRIT then shows a waiting screen while TCR verifies your business. This usually clears
within minutes to a couple of business days, and GRIT re-checks automatically — you
don't need to sit on the page.

## Step 2 — Message campaign

Once your business is verified, TCR wants to know *what* you'll send:

1. Write a short, plain description — for example: *"We send SMS appointment reminders
   and two-way messages to patients who opted in through our patient portal."*
2. Pick your primary opt-in method (portal checkbox, paper intake, verbal, or text
   keyword).
3. Click **Submit Campaign**.

GRIT registers you as **Low Volume Mixed** — the right choice for almost every solo
practice: cheaper, faster to approve, with a 2,000-messages/day ceiling you'll never
hit. Campaign review typically takes a few business days, occasionally up to two weeks.
Again: GRIT polls the status for you.

## Step 3 — Pick your number

When the campaign is approved, search available **local numbers** by area code and
claim one. That number is yours — patients see it on every text and can reply to it.

## Turn on reminders

With your number active, the SMS settings page unlocks:

- **SMS enabled** — the master switch for the practice.
- **Short name** — how your practice signs its texts (e.g. "ABC Chiro").
- **Appointment reminders** — toggle them on and pick when they send: 24 hours,
  48 hours, and/or 1 week before the visit. Reminders are created and sent
  automatically; if an appointment is rescheduled or cancelled, its reminders follow.

## Patient consent

Texting is consent-based, and GRIT enforces it:

- A patient only receives texts after consent is **recorded on their chart** — open the
  patient's **Text** tab to opt them in (portal, paper, or verbal consent).
- **STOP, HELP, and START** keywords are handled automatically, including the required
  confirmation messages. A STOP is honored instantly, no staff action needed.
- Every consent and opt-out is stored with how and when it happened — your audit trail
  if a carrier or patient ever asks.

## Troubleshooting

- **My business verification was rejected.** Nine times out of ten the legal name
  doesn't exactly match the IRS filing tied to your EIN. Fix the name (or the EIN) and
  resubmit — the form reopens with your previous answers.
- **It's been a week and my campaign is still pending.** Annoying, but within normal
  range for carrier review. If it goes past two weeks, contact support and we'll chase
  it.
- **A patient says they never get reminders.** Check their **Text** tab: they need a
  recorded opt-in, and their reminder is only scheduled if the appointment was created
  while reminders were enabled.
